What I Do

I help women lose weight, build discipline, and step into the version of themselves they keep talking about — through 1:1 coaching, weekly accountability check-ins, and a structure that keeps you consistent even on your busiest weeks. Most of my clients start with body and weight-loss coaching, and layer in mindset or money coaching once the foundation is set.

The Three Pillars

Everything I coach on falls under one of three pillars.

01

Body

Weight loss, movement, and accountability. This is the core of what I do — structured check-ins, exercise accountability, and a sustainable plan so the results actually stick.

02

Mind

Confidence and mindset coaching. We work on the self-talk, the habits, and the beliefs that either move you forward or keep you stuck.

03

Income

Building a healthier relationship with money and your ability to earn it — because confidence isn't just physical, it's financial too.
