What I Do

I help women lose weight, build discipline, and step into the version of themselves they keep talking about — through 1:1 coaching, weekly accountability check-ins, and a structure that keeps you consistent even on your busiest weeks. Most of my clients start with body and weight-loss coaching, and layer in mindset or money coaching once the foundation is set.

About Me

Devyn Alexandrea — Personal & Accountability Coach

Hi, I'm Devyn. Not long ago I was 209 pounds and in the middle of my own rock bottom — getting sober, rebuilding my body, and figuring out who I was underneath all of it. I built my body back one workout at a time. I built my mind back one honest day at a time. Since then I've lost 70+ pounds, stayed sober, and built a life I used to just talk about wanting — including saying "I do" to the life I have now.

I don't coach from a textbook — I coach from having actually done the work: the check-ins I didn't want to show up for, the days discipline mattered more than motivation, and the slow, unglamorous process of becoming someone I'm proud of. My job now is to be the person in your corner who won't let you quit on yourself — the same way I refused to quit on me.

What's a Lifestyle Intensive?

A 2-hour in-person or virtual intensive built around the part of your life that needs hands-on support most — grocery shopping, post-weight-loss wardrobe help, confidence outings, gym orientation, meal-prep/routine reset, home organization, or another personalized session we design together.

Local travel within 15 miles included. Additional travel may be available for an added fee.
